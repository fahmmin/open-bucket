Open Bucket

Exit rules for your DeFi loan. An agent watches every Monad block and closes your position before liquidation, and the contract only lets it pay you.

The problem

A common move on Monad: borrow USDC against ETH on Aave, then put that USDC in a vault to earn yield. The loan lives in one protocol and the money that could repay it lives in another.

Aave sees2 WETH collateral2,600 USDC debt
Nobody sees both sides
The vault sees2,574 USDC depositedNo idea there is a loan

When ETH drops, Aave only sees the loan. It liquidates the position and the liquidator keeps a 5 to 10% bonus, while the USDC that could have repaid the loan sits untouched in the vault.

Open Bucket puts the loan and the vault deposit inside one contract that belongs to you. You set exit rules, such as "exit if health falls below 1.15" or "exit by Friday". When a rule is true, anyone can trigger the exit: the contract pulls the USDC out of the vault, repays Aave, and returns your ETH to you.

What we are building today

Four small pieces. The labels say where each one runs during the demo.

Bucket contract

Live on Monad testnet

Holds your Aave loan and your vault deposit together. Stores your exit rules. Has one exit function that anyone can call once a rule is true, and it always pays the owner.

Routes

Live rates

The loan-plus-vault combinations we support, with live rates. Each route lists every step, contract and risk before you sign anything.

Agent

Open source, anyone can run it

A small program that checks every bucket on every block and calls exit when a rule is true. It earns a small capped tip for doing it. It cannot change where the money goes.

Dashboard

Web app

One screen for the whole position: what it holds, what it owes, its health, your rules, and a log of every decision the agent made.

What this route will do

  1. Supply 2 WETH to Aave
  2. Borrow 2,600 USDC from Aave
  3. Keep 26 USDC in the bucket and deposit 2,574 USDC in the vault

How the exit works

  1. Withdraw USDC from the vault
  2. Repay the Aave loan
  3. Withdraw your WETH
  4. Send everything to you, minus a capped tip if someone else triggered it

Risks we show you

  • The vault may not have enough cash for an instant withdrawal
  • Prices come from Chainlink and Aave's oracle
  • Smart contract risk in Aave, the vault and our contract

    Why this belongs on Monad

    The honest answer to "could this run on another chain?" is yes, on any EVM chain with Aave. We are building it here because the money and the gap are here.

    Lending is most of Monad DeFi: about $788M of roughly $912M TVL.DefiLlama, 3 Nov 2026
    No position automation is live on Monad mainnet. DeFi Saver, Summer.fi and Instadapp are not deployed, and Gelato Web3 Functions are testnet only.Checked 3 Nov 2026
    Blocks every 300 ms with 600 ms finality mean the agent acts in the next block, so the price moves less between your rule firing and your exit landing.Our reasoning

    Who does what today

    Everyone does a piece. Every lender built its own permission system, and none spans two protocols, so a loan on Aave with yield on Morpho has nobody watching both sides. From our research on 3 and 4 November 2026.

    ProductOn MonadLoan and yield togetherThe limit for this job
    DeFi SaverNoNo, loan onlyAllowlisted bots manage the loan (stop-loss, repay); they never touch your outside yield.
    Summer.fiNoNo, loan onlyStop-loss and trailing stop on the loan side only.
    InstadappNoNoSmart accounts for DeFi, not deployed on Monad.
    GearboxYes, $0.25MYesClosest model, but the debt must come from Gearbox's own pools. You can't bring your Aave loan.
    CurvanceYesNoleverageFor / deleverageFor delegation works inside Curvance only.
    MorphoYesNoPre-liquidation and Bundler3 cover Morpho markets, one transaction at a time.
    EulerYesNoEVC operators act on Euler vaults only.
    AaveYes, V3NoCredit delegation only; V4 Position Managers are governance-gated and not on Monad.
    Enso, LI.FIYesNoOne-transaction zaps across protocols; nothing watches the position afterwards.
    Gelato, FastLaneTestnet / unclearn/aKeeper infrastructure, not a product. Gelato Web3 Functions are testnet-only; FastLane's mainnet status is unknown.
    DefiLlama Yieldsn/aNoLists yields; doesn't net the borrow cost, open a position or protect it.
    Open BucketYes, testnetYesAave is the only lender today and there are no swaps yet. Next: flash-loan exits and more lenders.

    Left out on purpose

    • No AI model moves money. The agent follows your rules and nothing else. That is the safe kind of agent for a loan.
    • No token and no points.
    • Aave is the only lender in the MVP. Any ERC-4626 vault works on the yield side.
    • No swaps in the MVP. If the vault returns less than the debt, exit stops and says why. Rounding dust up to 0.01 USDC is handled. Next: flash-loan exits, which Aave Monad supports at 0.05%.
    • No leverage loops.

    Keeping the demo honest

    • Real Aave on a fork of Monad mainnet, labelled on screen: the routes, our bucket and the crash. We can't move ETH's real price, so we change the oracle price on a local copy of mainnet with the real Aave market and a real Morpho vault.
    • Live on Monad testnet: the same contracts and agent on a public chain. Aave isn't deployed on Monad testnet, so lending there runs on our Aave-compatible test pool, labelled in the app.
    • Backup: a screen recording of a full rehearsal, introduced as a recording if the Wi-Fi or RPC fails.

    Contract tests, on a fork of Monad mainnet

    • Open puts MON on Aave, records the loan and deposits USDC in the vault
    • The owner can exit at any time and gets everything back
    • A stranger cannot exit while no rule is true
    • After the deadline, or below the health floor, a stranger can exit. The money still goes to the owner and the stranger gets only the capped tip
    • Exit twice fails cleanly; exit with too little in the vault stops with a clear error
    • Only the owner can change rules or rescue tokens
    • Fuzz: random price drops and waits never leave the owner with less than the position's value minus the tip

    Agent and app checks

    • Agent dry-run mode logs decisions without sending anything
    • At most one exit per bucket; reconnects after an RPC drop
    • Explicit gas limit on every transaction, because Monad charges for the gas limit, not gas used
    • Every number on screen matches a direct contract read. USDC has 6 decimals, MON has 18
    • Wrong-network prompt, plus loading, empty and error states
    • The fork banner shows whenever the app points at the local fork
    • Readable on a projector: 1920×1080 at 125% zoom
